Project

AI-Based Structural Crack and Defect Detection System. Detects potholes, alligator cracks, longitudinal cracks, and transverse cracks from road surface images using two YOLOv8 models. Returns root causes, prevention measures, repair steps, and severity rating.

Research papers reviewed

[1] Maeda et al. (2018) — Road Damage Detection Using Deep Neural Networks
Computer-Aided Civil and Infrastructure Engineering, Vol. 33 · arXiv:1801.09454
Introduced the Road Damage Dataset (RDD2018) with 9,053 images. Used SSD framework for real-time detection. Foundational paper for road crack detection benchmarks.
[2] Arya et al. (2022) — RDD2022: A Multi-National Image Dataset for Road Damage Detection
arXiv:2209.08538 · Geoscience Data Journal, 2024
Introduced RDD2022 — 47,420 road images from six countries including India, with the exact four damage types this project detects. Primary dataset reference.
[3] Zuo et al. (2025) — Intelligent Road Crack Detection Based on Improved YOLOv8
arXiv:2504.13208 · April 2025
Enhanced YOLOv8 with ECA and CBAM attention mechanisms for road crack detection. Directly justifies our choice of YOLOv8 as the detection backbone.
[4] Dharneeshkar et al. (2020) — Detection of Potholes in Indian Roads Using YOLO
ICICT 2020 · IEEE
Applied YOLO to pothole detection specifically on Indian roads. Supports use of YOLO-based detection for this project targeting Kerala road conditions.
[5] Sandler et al. (2018) — MobileNetV2: Inverted Residuals and Linear Bottlenecks
CVPR 2018 · IEEE
Introduced MobileNetV2 lightweight CNN architecture. Used as secondary classification backbone in the Colab training notebook.
